Poem XIV

lastly, my love the happiest birthday to you i know this isnt really the best i can do, i rushed alot. wrote a few on the cruise. couldnt write it all on paper. ripped and threw away the paper in the bin, i wish i could do something real. had so many things in my mind so many ideas, so many gifts i could do. But here it is. You used to mention that you made a website for your ex so i thought maybe i could do the same for you and make up what he didn't do and maybe what you wanted was to be treater the same way you treated him. i love you and i hope you have the bestest birthday baby.
